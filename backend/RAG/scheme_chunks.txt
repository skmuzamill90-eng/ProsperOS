

PM SVANidhi

Description:
PM SVANidhi provides working-capital support to eligible street vendors.

Suitable for:
Eligible street vendors.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must meet the official street-vendor eligibility requirements.
- Other official conditions may apply.

Important:
This scheme should not be recommended solely because someone has a low income.

Skill India

Description:
Skill India provides skill-development and vocational training opportunities.

Suitable for:
Students, workers, job seekers and people who want to improve their skills.

Eligibility:
- Eligibility depends on the specific training program.
- Age, education and other requirements may vary.

Important:
The user should verify the current eligibility requirements for the selected program.

PM Mudra Yojana

Description:
PM Mudra Yojana supports eligible micro and small business activities through appropriate financing.

Suitable for:
Small business owners, self-employed people and eligible micro entrepreneurs.

Eligibility:
- Eligibility depends on the specific loan and applicant requirements.
- Business and lending conditions may apply.

Important:
The scheme should be considered when the user has an eligible business or self-employment activity.
